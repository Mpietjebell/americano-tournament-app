# Brag plan: two vertical videos

**The idea:** every player can host from their own phone. One video speaks to the person hosting, the other to the person invited. Both end on "every player can host".
**Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, 120 BPM (one beat is 0.5 s). Readable content stays between y 250 and 1536, and left of x 940 below mid-height, so it clears the Reels, TikTok and Shorts overlays.
**Type:** every line meant to be read is set in bold Libre Franklin (the app's sans-serif), at 88 px or larger for headlines. The thin Bodoni display face appears only in the NOPA wordmark.
**Data:** all player names are fictional stand-ins. The scores and standings come from a real test run of the app.
